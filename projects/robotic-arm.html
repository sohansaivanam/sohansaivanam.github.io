The challenge

The project called for moving a tennis ball six feet and placing it in a one-meter box. The mechanism had to combine travel along a linear slide with the motion needed to position and release the ball.

Design & integration

The build brought together aluminum extrusions, servo motors, a belt-driven linear slide, and an Arduino Uno R3. SolidWorks supported the mechanical design, while 3D-printed parts connected the custom geometry with the purchased components.

My contributions

My work included SolidWorks modeling, 3D-printed components, engineering drawings with GD&T, an EasyEDA schematic, and C-language control. This page will be expanded with the specific parts I owned and the decisions behind them.

What comes next on this page

The next additions are the actual CAD assembly, prototype photos, a motion demonstration, and a clear account of test results. These will show how the mechanism performed and what I would change in another iteration.
