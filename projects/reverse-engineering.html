The objective

Recreate a physical component as a usable 3D solid model from optical-scanner point-cloud data. This work was part of my MAE 1351 Honors Contract at UT Arlington.

My work

I processed the scanned geometry in SolidWorks ScanTo3D and reconstructed the component’s features to create a complete solid model.

The work connected CAD modeling with scanner-based metrology: interpreting measured surface data and rebuilding the geometry as a component model.
