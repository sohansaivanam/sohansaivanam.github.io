The direction

I am developing a compact two-wheeled biped as a way to bring mechanical design, actuation, and controls together in one project. The current architecture uses a hip actuator, knee actuator, and wheel motor on each side.

The design questions

The main questions are how to choose the leg geometry, size the actuators, package the mechanisms, and keep the build within a practical student budget. The work so far is focused on architecture and component tradeoffs.

Controls to explore

I am learning about PID and LQR stabilization while working through the mechanical design. These are areas of study for the project; successful balancing hardware has not yet been demonstrated.
