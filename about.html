Hi, I’m Sohan.

Mechanical engineering student. Curious builder. Usually thinking about the next thing to figure out.

A personal photo will go here.

I’m studying Mechanical Engineering at the University of Texas at Arlington, where I’m also part of the Honors College.

I’m interested in the path from an idea to a physical mechanism: choosing an architecture, working through the CAD, making parts, and seeing what happens when it all comes together.

My projects have brought me into robotics, Arduino integration, simulation, and payload planning. I’ve also joined the FSAE suspension team as a volunteer and am learning the foundations of FEA.
